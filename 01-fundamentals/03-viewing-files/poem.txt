The command line waits in silence,
A blinking cursor, patient and still.
No icons, no menus, no guidance —
Just you, the shell, and your will.

You type a word, the system responds,
Files appear like stars in the night.
Each keystroke a step, each output a bond
Between human thought and digital light.

So fear not the terminal's empty face,
For behind it lies power untold.
With practice, you'll navigate this space
Like the sysadmins of old.
